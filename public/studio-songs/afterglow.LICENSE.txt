This song package is an original hackathon demonstration created for Signal Studio.
It includes the composition, English lyrics, melody, multitrack MIDI arrangement,
karaoke timing, and the browser-rendered instrumental recording.

No third-party song, lyric sheet, tablature, MIDI transcription, master recording,
performer voice, album artwork, or music video was copied into this package. Genre
conventions such as a late-1990s/early-2000s pop tempo, clean guitar arpeggios,
electric keys, pads, and a chorus lift are not source assets.

The package is distributed with the Signal Studio source solely as an original
project-authored demonstration. Third-party reuse requires permission from the
copyright owner.
